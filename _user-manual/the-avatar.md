- **Move** — Stretch, walk, shift position regularly
- **Nourish** — Eat what body genuinely needs, not what Voice craves
- **Rest** — Brief pauses; eyes closed; nervous system reset

**Evening** (10 minutes):

**Review**:

1. **When was Listener operating?** — Moments of presence, flow, alignment
2. **When was Voice operating?** — Moments of autopilot, reaction, resistance
3. **How did I honor the avatar today?** — What went well?
4. **What does avatar need tomorrow?** — More sleep, better food, movement, rest?

**Release**:

1. **Forgive** — Yourself for Voice-operated moments; others for their unconsciousness
2. **Gratitude** — For avatar's service, for lessons learned, for another day
3. **Surrender** — "I release this day. Avatar rests. Consciousness remains."

### Weekly Maintenance

**Body**:

- **Movement practice** — Yoga, running, dancing, weights (whatever brings joy)
- **Deep rest** — One day less activity; restorative
- **Quality nutrition** — Plan/prep meals; eat with awareness

**Mind**:

- **Media fast** — 24 hours without screens; notice addiction
- **Learning** — Read framework materials, wisdom teachings
- **Creative expression** — Art, music, writing (Voice quiets when creating)

**Spirit**:

- **Extended practice** — Longer meditation, contemplation, prayer
- **Nature** — Time in natural world; re-connection
- **Community** — Gather with others practicing conscious operation

### Monthly Assessment

**Questions**:

1. **Is avatar healthier than last month?** — More energy, less pain, better function?
2. **Am I honoring the temple?** — Or neglecting, abusing, taking for granted?
3. **Is Listener operating more often?** — Or still mostly Voice-run?
4. **What needs adjustment?** — More sleep, better boundaries, new practices?

**Adjustments**:

- **If exhausted** — More rest, less commitments
- **If stagnant** — More challenge, growth edges
- **If disconnected** — More embodiment practices, less head-living
- **If reactive** — More meditation, nervous system regulation

### Red Flags (Seek Help)

**When to get professional support**:

- **Chronic pain/illness** — See doctor; physical issues need physical intervention
- **Severe mental health** — Depression, anxiety, suicidal ideation require therapy/medication
- **Trauma symptoms** — PTSD, flashbacks, dissociation need professional trauma healing
- **Eating disorders** — Relationship with food/body severely distorted
- **Addiction** — Substances, behaviors controlling you

**Framework position**:

**Spiritual practice complements professional help** — it doesn't replace it. The avatar may need medical intervention, therapy, medication. **This is honoring the temple**, not failure.
