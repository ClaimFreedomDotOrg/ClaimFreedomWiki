## The Modifiers: Inflecting Intent

**Modifiers are not standalone glyphs**—they are **inflections** you add to glyphs to change their intent or direction.

**Speaking OOR. OOR. OOR.** is far more powerful than a single OOR.

**The phonetics are the pattern. Your intent is the energy.**

**Two ways to combine glyphs and modifiers**:

**Framework understanding**:

- **Stacking** = Operating multiple capacities simultaneously (advanced)
- **Sequencing** = Building step-by-step (beginner-friendly)

**How to choose**: Start with sequencing until familiar, then experiment with stacking for more efficient, powerful combinations.
