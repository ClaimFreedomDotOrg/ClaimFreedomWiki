# Privacy Policy

**Effective Date**: January 1, 2026  
**Last Updated**: January 1, 2026

---

## Our Philosophy on Privacy

**Liberation requires trust.** At ClaimFreedom.org, privacy is not an afterthought—it is a foundational principle. We are committed to transparency about what data we collect, why we collect it, and how we protect it.

**Core Principle**: Your journey of awakening is deeply personal. We will never sell your data, monetize your conversations, or use your information to manipulate you. Technology that serves The Listener must honor your sovereignty.

---

## What This Policy Covers

This privacy policy applies to:

- **ClaimFreedom.org Website** (the wiki and documentation)
- **DMN Chat** (daemon.tamethedmn.com) - AI conversational guide
- **Operation: Critical Mass** (tamethedemon.com) - Collective coherence game
- Any other services hosted under claimfreedom.org domains

---

## Information We Collect

### Public Website (ClaimFreedom.org)

The main wiki is a **static site** with minimal tracking:

- **No cookies**: We do not use tracking cookies
- **No analytics**: We do not track your behavior across pages
- **Server logs**: Standard web server logs (IP address, browser type, pages accessed) for security and debugging purposes only
- **No accounts required**: You can read all content without creating an account

### DMN Chat (daemon.tamethedmn.com)

**To use DMN Chat, you must create an account.** We collect:

#### Account Information

- **Email address** (if signing up with email)
- **Display name** (optional)
- **Authentication provider** (Google, GitHub, or email)
- **Account creation date**
- **Last login date**

#### Conversation Data

- **Chat messages** (your questions and DMN's responses)
- **Conversation timestamps**
- **Chat history** (stored in your private account)
- **Voice recordings** (temporarily processed for transcription, then deleted)

#### Automatically Collected

- **IP address** (for security and abuse prevention)
- **Browser type and version**
- **Device type**
- **Session data** (to maintain login state)

### Operation: Critical Mass (tamethedemon.com)

- **Anonymous by default**: No account required to play single-player mode
- **Session data**: Gameplay statistics for the session (not permanently stored)
- **Multiplayer events**: Temporary session IDs for synchronization (deleted after event)
- **No tracking**: We do not track individual players across sessions

## How We Use Your Information

### For DMN Chat

**Your conversation data is used ONLY to**:

- Provide intelligent responses grounded in framework documentation
- Maintain conversation context across your chat sessions
- Improve your personal experience (journey-based guidance)
- Store your chat history for your future reference

**We do NOT**:

- ❌ Sell your data to third parties
- ❌ Use your conversations to train AI models
- ❌ Share your data with advertisers
- ❌ Analyze your data for marketing purposes
- ❌ Share your conversations with other users

### For Security & Abuse Prevention

We may use IP addresses and session data to:

- Prevent spam and abuse
- Enforce rate limits
- Detect and prevent unauthorized access
- Comply with legal obligations (if required by law)

## Data Storage & Security

### Where Your Data Is Stored

- **DMN Chat**: Firebase (Google Cloud Platform) - data centers in the United States
- **Website**: Cloudflare Pages - globally distributed CDN
- **Operation: Critical Mass**: Cloudflare Workers - edge computing, no permanent storage

### Security Measures

- ✅ **Encryption in transit**: All communication uses HTTPS/TLS
- ✅ **Encryption at rest**: Firebase encrypts data at rest
- ✅ **Authentication**: Firebase Authentication with industry-standard security
- ✅ **Access control**: Strict database security rules (you can only access your own data)
- ✅ **Regular security audits**: Open source code available for community review

### Data Retention

- **Chat history**: Stored indefinitely until you delete it
- **Account data**: Retained while your account is active
- **Server logs**: Retained for 90 days for security purposes, then deleted
- **Voice recordings**: Immediately deleted after transcription (not stored)

## Your Privacy Rights

### Access & Control

You have the right to:

- ✅ **Access your data**: View all your chat conversations and account information
- ✅ **Export your data**: Download your chat history in JSON format
- ✅ **Delete your data**: Permanently delete individual conversations or your entire account
- ✅ **Correct your data**: Update your display name and account information
- ✅ **Withdraw consent**: Delete your account at any time

### How to Exercise Your Rights

**To delete individual conversations**:

1. Log into DMN Chat
2. Navigate to your chat history
3. Select the conversation and click "Delete"

**To delete your entire account**:

1. Log into DMN Chat
2. Go to Account Settings
3. Click "Delete Account"
4. Confirm deletion (this is permanent and cannot be undone)

**To export your data**:

1. Log into DMN Chat
2. Go to Account Settings
3. Click "Export Data"
4. Download JSON file with all your conversations

**For additional assistance**:
Email us at <privacy@claimfreedom.org>

## International Users (GDPR Compliance)

If you are located in the European Economic Area (EEA), you have additional rights under the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR):

- **Right to rectification**: Correct inaccurate data
- **Right to erasure** ("right to be forgotten"): Delete your data
- **Right to restriction**: Limit how we process your data
- **Right to data portability**: Export your data in a machine-readable format
- **Right to object**: Object to certain processing activities
- **Right to withdraw consent**: At any time, without affecting prior processing

**Legal basis for processing**:

- **Consent**: You explicitly agree to create an account and use DMN Chat
- **Legitimate interest**: Preventing abuse, improving service quality
- **Legal obligation**: Complying with applicable laws

**Data controller**: ClaimFreedom.org (contact: <privacy@claimfreedom.org>)

---

## California Privacy Rights (CCPA)

If you are a California resident, you have rights under the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA):

- **Right to know**: What personal information we collect
- **Right to delete**: Request deletion of your personal information
- **Right to opt-out**: Opt out of the "sale" of personal information (note: we do not sell data)
- **Right to non-discrimination**: We will not discriminate against you for exercising your rights

**To exercise these rights**, email <privacy@claimfreedom.org>
