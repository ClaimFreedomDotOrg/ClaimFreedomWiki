DMN Chat includes **voice conversation** — you can speak your questions naturally and hear responses read aloud.

### Why Voice Matters

**Neurologically**: Speaking engages different neural pathways than typing. Voice conversation:

- Activates language processing centers more directly
- Feels more natural and conversational
- Reduces cognitive load (no typing required)
- Enables practice while walking, resting, or in contemplation

**Philosophically**: The ancients transmitted wisdom through **oral tradition**. Voice carries presence that text alone cannot. Hearing teachings spoken can create resonance that reading doesn't.

### How It Works

1. **Speak your question**: Press and hold the microphone button
2. **Real-time transcription**: See your words as you speak (live feedback)
3. **Intelligent response**: The AI searches the framework and formulates an answer
4. **Voice playback**: The response is read aloud with natural speech synthesis
5. **Continue the conversation**: Ask follow-up questions naturally

**Use cases**:

- Morning contemplation while making coffee
- Walking meditation with voice guidance
- Lying in stillness without needing to look at a screen
- Accessibility for those who prefer or require voice interaction
