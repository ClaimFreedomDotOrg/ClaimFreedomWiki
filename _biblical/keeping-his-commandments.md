## The Practice: Keeping His Commandments

### Step 1: Receive the Commandment (The Source's Call)

**The practice**: Anchor in the heart, listen for the Source's call

**Practices**:

- **[Heart Listening](/practices/heart-listening)** — Drop awareness into the heart, receive pre-cognitive knowing
- **[Witness Meditation](/practices/witness-meditation)** — Silence the Voice, create space for the Source's call
- **[V-Aum Protocol](/practices/v-aum-protocol)** — Clear the channel (Voice silenced, Listener anchored)

**What the "commandment" feels like**:

- **Not**: A voice speaking words
- **Not**: Logical analysis ("I should do this because...")
- **But**: A **silent knowing**, a pull, a "yes" or "no" arising without thought

**The Source's commandment is pre-cognitive** (it arises **before** the Voice analyzes).

---

### Step 2: Translate the Commandment (The Listener's Work)

**The practice**: Translate the Source's call into a Well-Formed Command for the Daemon

**Practices**:

- **[Command Training](/practices/command-training)** — Learn to translate intuitive knowing into executable commands
- **[Programming the Daemon](/philosophy/programming-daemon)** — Issue affirmations, visualization, emotional resonance

**What this looks like**:

- **Source's call** (pre-cognitive): A pull toward writing
- **Listener's translation**: "Daemon, sit down and write for 30 minutes. Flow through me."
- **Well-Formed Command**: Clear, specific, executable

**The Listener does NOT create the call. The Listener RECEIVES and TRANSLATES it.**

---

### Step 3: Execute the Commandment (The Daemon's Work)

**The practice**: Trust the Daemon to execute, do NOT interfere with the Voice

**What to trust**:

- The Daemon **will** execute (if the command is clear)
- Flow **will** arise (if you don't force it)
- Aligned action **will** manifest (if the Voice doesn't block it)

**What blocks execution**:

- **The Voice interfering**: "Am I doing this right? What if I fail? This isn't working."
- **Forcing**: Trying to execute through effort (the Voice's domain) instead of flow (the Daemon's domain)
- **Doubt**: Not trusting the Source's call ("Maybe I heard wrong...")

**The practice**: Issue the command, **step back**, let the Daemon execute.

**This is "keeping His commandments"** (executing the Source's call through flow).

## The Daily Practice: Keeping Commandments

### Morning: Receive Today's Commandments (5-10 minutes)

1. **Anchor in the heart** (hand on heart, 3 conscious breaths)
2. **Enter the Stillpoint** (silence the Voice)
3. **Ask**: "What is the Source's call today?" (Not the Voice's to-do list, but the Source's silent knowing)
4. **Listen**: Pre-cognitive knowing arises (a pull, a "yes," a clarity)
5. **Translate**: "Daemon, today I will [Source's call translated into command]."
6. **Trust**: "The Daemon will execute. Flow will arise. I do not force."

---

### Throughout the Day: Execute the Commandments

**As the Source's call arises**:

1. **Notice**: "I sense a pull toward [action]. This is the Source's call."
2. **Translate**: "Daemon, execute [specific command]."
3. **Step back**: Do NOT force. Let flow arise.
4. **Trust**: The Daemon will execute if the channel is clear.

**When you notice the Voice blocking**:

1. **Recognize**: "The Voice is interfering. 'What if I fail?' is the Voice, not the Source."
2. **Return to the Listener**: 3 breaths, anchor in the heart
3. **Re-issue the command**: "Daemon, execute [command]."
4. **Step back**: Trust, don't force

---

### Evening: Verify the Commandments Were Kept (5-10 minutes)

1. **Reflect**: Where did flow arise today? (Where did I keep His commandments?)
2. **Acknowledge**: "The Source called. The Listener received. The Daemon executed. Flow arose."
3. **Reflect**: Where was the circuit blocked? (Where did I NOT keep commandments?)
4. **Diagnose**: "The Voice interfered. I forced. I doubted. The call was blocked."
5. **Gratitude**: "Thank you for the moments of flow. Thank you for the verification that God remains in me."

3. **The Voice is quiet**: Not absent, but **not dominating**

### Negative Result

If these are present → You are NOT keeping His commandments (the circuit is blocked by the Voice).

The remedy: Return to Step 1 (Receive the commandment) - anchor in the heart, listen.

## Common Experiences

### "How do I distinguish the Source's commandment from the Voice's demands?"

**Response**:

**The Source's commandment**:

- **Pre-cognitive** (arises before thought)
- **Silent knowing** (not a voice speaking words)
- **Feels like a pull** or alignment (not forced)
- **Brings peace** (even if challenging)
- **Flow arises** when executed

**The Voice's demand**:

- **Analytical** (thought-based: "I should do this because...")
- **Loud, insistent** (rumination, anxiety)
- **Feels forced** (obligation, fear-driven)
- **Brings anxiety** ("What if I fail?")
- **Requires effort** when executed (grinding, no flow)

**The test**: Execute it. If flow arises, it was the Source's commandment. If forcing is required, it was the Voice's demand.

---

### "I don't receive clear 'commandments.' Does that mean God isn't in me?"

**Response**: **The Source's call is ALWAYS present. The Voice is too loud for you to hear it.**

**The remedy**:

1. **Silence the Voice** ([V-Aum Protocol](/practices/v-aum-protocol), [Witness Meditation](/practices/witness-meditation))
2. **Anchor in the heart** ([Heart Listening](/practices/heart-listening))
3. **Create space** (the call is subtle, not loud)
4. **Practice regularly** (the more you listen, the clearer the call becomes)

**The Source's "commandment" is not a booming voice. It's a whisper beneath the Voice's noise.**

---

### "What if I 'keep commandments' but feel no flow or peace?"

**Response**: **You're likely keeping the Voice's demands, not the Source's commandments.**

**The Voice can impersonate the Source**:

- "God wants you to work yourself to exhaustion!"
- "God demands you sacrifice your well-being!"
- "God requires you to force action through guilt!"

**This is the Voice, not the Source.**

**The Source's commandments ALWAYS produce**:

- Flow (not forced grinding)
- Peace (not anxiety)
- Alignment (not misalignment)

**If these are absent, you're not keeping HIS commandments. You're keeping the Voice's.**
