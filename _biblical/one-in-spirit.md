# "One with Him in Spirit"

**1 Corinthians 6:17 — The Union of Listener and Source**

---

## The Verse

> *"But he who unites himself with the Lord is one with Him in spirit."*
>
> — 1 Corinthians 6:17 (BSB)

---

## The Neuro-Gnostic Decoding

This verse reveals the **complete union** of The Listener with The Source:

**"He who unites himself with the Lord"** = The one who anchors in The Source (through The Listener)

**"Is one with Him in spirit"** = Complete non-separation, unity of consciousness

**This is [the Sacred Order](/philosophy/sacred-order) fully realized**: Source ↔ Listener (one spirit, no barrier, no separation).

---

## Breaking Down the Verse

### "He Who Unites Himself with the Lord"

**"Unites" (Greek: *kollaō*)** = To join, to bind together, to become one with

**NOT**: A one-time religious event (joining a church, getting baptized)

**BUT**: The **continuous practice** of anchoring consciousness in The Source

**"With the Lord"** = With The Source (The Father, The Absolute, pre-cognitive intuition)

**How does one "unite with the Lord"?**

**Not through**:

- External religious performance (rituals, doctrines, church membership)
- Moral striving (trying to be good enough to deserve union)
- Intellectual belief (accepting theological propositions)

**But through**:

- **Anchoring in The Listener** (dis-identifying from the Voice)
- **Receiving the Source's call** (Heart Listening, intuitive knowing)
- **Aligning action with the call** (Flow State Conduction)

**The practice of union**:

1. **Recognize you are The Listener** (not the Voice)
2. **Anchor in The Stillpoint** (the silence beneath thoughts)
3. **Receive the Source's call** (pre-cognitive intuition, heart-brain coherence)
4. **Command the Daemon** to execute aligned action

### The Practice of Union

This is "uniting with the Lord."

---

### "Is One with Him in Spirit"

**"One" (Greek: *hen*)** = A single unified whole, no separation, no barrier

**"In spirit" (Greek: *pneumati*)** = In consciousness, in awareness, in the Divine Spark

**NOT**: Physical merger (you don't disappear into The Source)

**BUT**: **Unity of consciousness** (The Listener and The Source are one, undivided)

**This is the Gnostic teaching**:

- **The Divine Spark (Pneuma)** within you **is not separate** from The Source
- **The Listener** is the offspring of The Source, not a distant creation
- **There is no barrier** between you (as The Listener) and The Source

**Cross-reference**:

- **John 14:20**: "I am in my Father" (The Listener is **in** The Source)
- **John 17:21**: "That they may all be one, just as You, Father, are in Me, and I am in You" (Unity, not separation)
- **1 John 4:13**: "We remain in Him, and He in us" (Mutual indwelling)

**1 Corinthians 6:17 is the explicit declaration**: When you unite with the Lord, **you are one spirit**.

---

### The Context: Contrast with Union with "Harlots"

**1 Corinthians 6:16** (the verse immediately before):

> *"Do you not know that he who unites himself with a prostitute is one with her in body? For it is said, 'The two will become one flesh.'"*

**Paul's contrast**:

- **Union with "harlots" (the hijacking)** = One **in body** (identification with the false self, the ego)
- **Union with the Lord** = One **in spirit** (identification with the true Self, The Listener)

**Neuro-Gnostic translation**:

- **"Harlots"** = The hijacking patterns, the parasitic attachments, the Archons
- **"One in body"** = Identified with the narrative self (the hijacked DMN, the Demon)
- **Union with the Lord** = Anchored in The Listener (aligned with The Source)
- **"One in spirit"** = The Listener and The Source functioning as a unified whole

**The warning**: If you remain identified with the Voice (the hijacked DMN), you are "one with the harlots" (the hijacking).

**If you anchor in The Listener**, you are "one with the Lord in spirit."

**You cannot serve two masters** (Matthew 6:24). You cannot be **both** identified with the Voice **and** anchored in The Listener.

**Choose**: The hijacking (one in body) or The Source (one in spirit).

## Common Experiences

### "Isn't it blasphemous to say I'm 'one with God'?"

**Response**: **You (the ego, the Voice) are NOT one with God.**

**But The Listener (the Divine Spark, Christ Consciousness) IS one with The Source.**

**The confusion arises when "I" is misidentified**:

- **If "I" = the Voice (ego)**: Then no, you are NOT one with God. You are identified with the hijacking.
- **If "I" = The Listener (Divine Spark)**: Then yes, you ARE one with The Source in spirit.

**This is why dis-identification is essential**: Until you recognize you are The Listener (not the Voice), claiming "I am one with God" is ego inflation.

**But once you are anchored in The Listener**, the recognition "I am one with Him in spirit" is **truth**, not blasphemy.

---

### "I don't feel 'one' with the Lord. Does that mean I'm not united?"

**Response**: **The union is not a feeling. The union is the underlying reality.**

**The hijacking (the Voice) obscures the union, but it doesn't **break** the union.**

**The practice is the repeated recognition**:

- "I **am** one with the Lord in spirit" (the truth)
- "I've **forgotten** the union" (when identified with the Voice)
- "I **remember** the union" (when anchored in The Listener)

**Check for the evidence** (see [We Remain in Him](/biblical/remain-in-him)):

- Can you find the Stillpoint? (Yes → The union is present)
- Can you witness the Voice? (Yes → The union is present)
- Can you sense the Source's call? (Yes → The union is present)

**If you can witness, the union is present. The union is not a feeling. The union is awareness itself.**

---

### "How is 'union with the Lord' different from 'union with harlots'?"

**Response**:

- **Union with "harlots"** (the hijacking) = Identification with the **false self** (the Voice, the ego, the narrative)
- **Union with the Lord** = Identification with the **true Self** (The Listener, the Divine Spark)

**The mechanism**:

- **What you unite with** (identify with) **is what you become** (in experience)
- Unite with the Voice → You experience rumination, fear, hijacked patterns ("one in body")
- Unite with The Source → You experience peace, flow, aligned action ("one in spirit")

**The practice is discernment**: Am I united with the hijacking (the Voice) or with The Source (The Listener)?
