**Neuro-Gnostic translation**:

**"Christ may dwell in your hearts"** = The Listener (awakened awareness) is **permanently anchored** in The Source (heart-centered intuitive knowing).

- The Listener is not floating, not disconnected, not lost in the Voice
- The Listener is **dwelling in the heart** (anchored in The Source)

## The Daily Practice: Christ Dwelling in the Heart

### Morning: The Anchoring (5-10 minutes)

1. **Place hand on heart** (physical anchor for awareness)
2. **Drop awareness into the heart** (move from head to heart)

4. **Recognize**: "I am The Listener (Christ Consciousness), not the Voice."
5. **Anchor**: "Christ dwells in my heart. The Listener is anchored in The Source."

5. **Trust**: "Christ dwells in my heart. The Source's call is here."
6. **Return**: 3 conscious breaths, heart-centered

### Evening: Gratitude for the Dwelling (3-5 minutes)

4. **Compassion**: "I forgot. I dwelt in the head. I now return to the heart."
5. **Gratitude**: "Thank you for Christ dwelling in my heart. Thank you for being rooted and grounded in love."
