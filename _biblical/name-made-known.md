# "I Have Made Your Name Known"

**John 17:26 — The Purpose of the Teaching: Transferring the Flow**

---

## The Verse

> *"And I have made Your name known to them and will continue to make it known, so that the love You have for Me may be in them, and I in them."*
>
> — John 17:26 (BSB)

---

## The Neuro-Gnostic Decoding

This is the **climax** of Jesus' High Priestly Prayer (John 17) — revealing **why** he taught, what he taught, and what the result would be.

**"I have made Your name known"** = I have revealed the nature of The Source (not external deity, but the Father **within**)

**"And will continue to make it known"** = The teaching continues (not finished, but ongoing through Gnosis)

**"So that the love You have for Me"** = The flow, the call, the sap (the Source's guidance to The Listener)

**"May be in them"** = May flow **through** them (not just observed in Jesus, but **activated** in all)

**"And I in them"** = Christ Consciousness (The Listener) functioning within each person

**This is the transfer protocol**: Making the Father's name known → Activates the flow (love) within → Christ (The Listener) indwells.

**NOT**: Worship Jesus externally as the **only** Son

**BUT**: Recognize Christ Consciousness **within** as **your true nature** (the Son within you)

## Breaking Down the Verse

### "I Have Made Your Name Known to Them"

**"Your name" (Greek: *to onoma sou*)** = The nature, identity, essence of The Source (not literal letters)

**In Hebrew tradition**: The Name = YHWH (I AM, the unnameable, the ground of being)

**"Made known" (Greek: *egnōrisa*)** = Revealed, disclosed, made experientially knowable (not intellectually described)

**Translation**: "I have revealed the **true nature** of The Source to them."

**What Jesus revealed about The Source (the Father)**:

1. **Not external** — "The kingdom of God is within you" (Luke 17:21)
2. **Not distant** — "I and the Father are one" (John 10:30)
3. **Not punitive** — "God is love" (1 John 4:8)
4. **Not exclusive** — "That they may be one as We are one" (John 17:21-23)
5. **Not separate** — "The Father is in Me, and I am in the Father" (John 10:38)

**The Name = The Source's true nature**: Present within, non-dual, loving flow, indwelling all.

**To "make the Name known" = To reveal [the Sacred Order](/philosophy/sacred-order)** (Source → Listener → Daemon, all within).

---

### "And Will Continue to Make It Known"

**"Will continue" (Greek: *gnōrisō*)** = Ongoing action, not completed in the past

**This is critical**: Jesus is **not** saying "I finished the job, now believe in me."

**Jesus IS saying**: "I revealed the pattern ([the Sacred Order](/philosophy/sacred-order), the indwelling), and this revelation **continues** (through Gnosis, direct experience)."

**How the revelation continues**:

1. **Through the practices** (Witness Meditation, Heart Listening, V-Aum Protocol)
2. **Through the works** (flow, fruit, evidence) — [Believe the Works](/biblical/believe-the-works)
3. **Through the Spirit** (the Source's call arising within) — [Keeping His Commandments](/biblical/keeping-his-commandments)
4. **Through mutual indwelling** (Christ in you, you in Christ) — [That They May Be One](/biblical/that-they-may-be-one)

**The revelation is NOT**: A historical event finished 2000 years ago

**The revelation IS**: An ongoing **experiential knowing** (Gnosis) available now

**When you experience**:

- The Stillpoint (silence, the Father's presence)
- The Witness (The Listener, Christ Consciousness)
- The Call (the Source's guidance from the heart)
- The Flow (aligned action arising)

**You are experiencing** "the Name being made known" (the revelation continuing).

---

### "So That the Love You Have for Me"

**"So that" (Greek: *hina*)** = Purpose clause (this is the **goal** of making the Name known)

**"The love You have for Me"** = The flow between Father (Source) and Son (Listener/Jesus)

**"Love" (Greek: *agapē*)** = Not sentimental emotion, but **flow state, alignment, creative power**

**This is the love**:

- The Source's call to The Listener (pre-cognitive knowing, intuitive guidance)
- The Listener's alignment with The Source (trust, receptivity, anchoring)
- The flow that arises from union (sap flowing through the vine → branch)

**In Jesus' experience**: The Father (Source) "loves" the Son (Listener/Jesus) = The Source's call flows perfectly to Jesus' Listener → Perfect alignment, flow, manifestation.

**This is the "love" that Jesus is describing** — not external affection, but **the flow of [the Sacred Order](/philosophy/sacred-order) functioning**.

---

### "May Be in Them"

**"In them" (Greek: *en autois*)** = Within them, activated within, flowing through them

**This is the transfer**: The love (flow) that functioned in Jesus → Now functions **in you**.

**NOT**: Observe Jesus' flow from outside ("Look how holy he is")

**BUT**: Activate the **same flow** within yourself ("The Father in me, I in the Father")

**How the love (flow) "is in them"**:

1. **The Source's call arises** within (pre-cognitive knowing, heart-brain coherence)
2. **The Listener receives** the call (Salience Network anchored, witnessing)
3. **The Daemon executes** the call (aligned action, flow, fruit)
4. **The flow manifests** (love is "in them" = [the Sacred Order](/philosophy/sacred-order) operational)

**This is what Jesus taught**: Not "worship me," but "the **same union** I have with the Father is **available to you**."

**John 17:21-23** ([That They May Be One](/biblical/that-they-may-be-one)):

> *"That they may be one, as We are one—I in them and You in Me—so that they may be perfectly united."*

**The love (flow) in Jesus** = The love (flow) available in you (when [the Sacred Order](/philosophy/sacred-order) is activated).

---

### "And I in Them"

**"I in them" (Greek: *kagō en autois*)** = Christ Consciousness (The Listener) functioning within each person

**This is the indwelling**: Not Jesus' physical body inside you, but **Christ (The Listener, Divine Spark) activated as your true nature**.

**"I" (Jesus speaking)**: Christ Consciousness, The Listener in perfect union with The Source.

**When Jesus says "I in them," he means**:

- **The Listener** (Christ, Divine Spark) is activated within you
- **The witnessing awareness** (Salience Network) is anchored
- **[The Sacred Order](/philosophy/sacred-order)** (Source → Listener → Daemon) is operational
- **You ARE The Listener** (not the Voice, not the ego)

**This is the recognition**: "Christ in you, the hope of glory" ([Colossians 1:27](/biblical/christ-in-you))

**The mystery revealed**: You are **not** the Voice (the hijacked DMN). You **are** The Listener (Christ Consciousness, the Divine Spark).

**When "I am in them" is functioning**:

- The Witness is present (observing thoughts, not identifying)
- The Stillpoint is accessible (silence, the Father's seat)
- The Call is received (intuitive knowing from the heart)
- The Flow arises (aligned action, fruit, manifestation)

**This is the goal** of "making the Name known" (revealing the Source's true nature): So that **the same indwelling** Jesus experienced → Activates in you.
