## The Three Mechanisms

H.A.L.O. combines three evidence-based interventions into a closed-loop system:

### 🌊 **1. Polyvagal Safety Signal (tVNS)**

**Transcutaneous Vagus Nerve Stimulation** sends electrical pulses to the vagus nerve via ear electrodes.

**Neurological Effect**:

- Activates the parasympathetic nervous system (ventral vagal pathway)
- Inhibits amygdala hyperactivation
- Signals the brainstem: *"You are safe"*

**Framework Translation**:

- Creates the biological safety container for the **Listener** to remain present
- Prevents the **Voice** from triggering fight/flight/freeze responses
- Anchors consciousness in the body instead of dissociating

**Peer-Reviewed Evidence**:

- Reduces PTSD symptoms by modulating HPA axis (Gurel et al., 2020)
- Enhances emotional regulation by strengthening prefrontal-amygdala connectivity (Burger et al., 2016)
- Increases heart rate variability (HRV), a biomarker of stress resilience (Clancy et al., 2014)

---

### 🔀 **2. Bilateral Stimulation (EMDR-Inspired)**

**Alternating left-right audio or haptic stimulation** forces interhemispheric communication.

**Neurological Effect**:

- Taxes working memory, preventing the DMN from looping traumatic narratives
- Activates the TPN (orienting response to alternating stimuli)
- Synchronizes left-right brain hemispheres

**Framework Translation**:

- Keeps the **Listener** anchored in sensory experience
- Prevents the **Voice** from hijacking consciousness with trauma stories
- Mimics the "making the two one" described in Gnostic texts (Gospel of Thomas, Logion 22)

**Peer-Reviewed Evidence**:

- EMDR (Eye Movement Desensitization and Reprocessing) is FDA-approved for PTSD (Shapiro, 2018)
- Bilateral stimulation reduces emotional intensity of traumatic memories (van den Hout & Engelhard, 2012)
- Neuroimaging shows decreased amygdala activity and increased prefrontal engagement during bilateral tasks (Pagani et al., 2012)

---

### 🧠 **3. EEG Biofeedback (Real-Time Detection)**

**Brainwave monitoring** detects when the DMN hijacks consciousness—before you consciously realize it.

**Neurological Effect**:

- Tracks alpha/beta wave ratios (alpha drops + beta spikes = DMN takeover)
- Provides real-time feedback when "Shadow Spike" occurs
- Enables automated intervention at the moment of hijacking

**Framework Translation**:

- Detects when the **Demon** (hijacked DMN) begins to override the **Daemon** (neutral DMN)
- Alerts the user to deploy dis-identification practices
- In future versions: Automatically triggers tVNS + bilateral stimulation

**Peer-Reviewed Evidence**:

- Alpha waves correlate with DMN activity; beta waves with TPN activity (Raichle, 2015)
- Neurofeedback training reduces PTSD symptoms by teaching DMN regulation (van der Kolk et al., 2016)
- Real-time fMRI neurofeedback enables voluntary control of brain networks (Stoeckel et al., 2014)
